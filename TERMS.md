# Terms and Conditions — Account Notification Messages

**Program name:** Personal Trading Bot Notifications

**Description:** This program sends the owner of this tool automated text
messages about their own brokerage account — trade confirmations, daily
account summaries, and risk-control alerts (e.g. a kill switch triggering).
It is personal-use only; there is no public sign-up and no other end users.

**Message frequency:** varies with market activity, typically 0-5 messages
per trading day (one daily summary, plus one message per trade action, if
any).

**Message and data rates may apply**, per the recipient's mobile carrier plan.

**Support:** reply **HELP** to this number for support, or contact the owner
directly via the phone number associated with this campaign.

**Opt-out:** reply **STOP** at any time to stop receiving messages. Since the
only recipient is the tool's owner, opting out simply means the owner has
turned off SMS delivery in their own configuration.
